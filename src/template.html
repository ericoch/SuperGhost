Rules and settings

Players take turns adding one letter anywhere in the row. Whoever completes a real word (of at least the minimum length) loses the round.

Instead of moving, you can challenge the player who just moved if you think no word contains the letters in their current order. They then have to name one. A real word means the challenger loses the round; no word means the challenged player loses.

Each lost round earns a letter of GHOST. Spell it out and you lose the game. Who starts the next round is a setting below; by default it's the loser.

Either colour can be played by the computer. Easy knows about 13,000 everyday words and sometimes plays carelessly; Medium knows about 36,000; Hard about 53,000; Perfect knows the whole dictionary and never makes a mistake. A computer only moves to letters it can complete with a word it knows, and challenges you when it can't think of one, so obscure words are your best weapon against the lower levels.

With the full dictionary, the second player can always win. Perfect will beat you whenever you start a round.

Keyboard: type a letter to pick it, ← → to choose a gap, Enter to place, Esc to clear.

Dictionary: the ENABLE word list (172,823 words).
